# CREATIVE QUALITY AUDIT

## 1. Executive Summary

This audit assesses the real-world creative output quality generated by the Mitra AI Marketing Engine. Instead of testing infrastructure (HTTP 200s), this audit evaluates the semantic, strategic, and linguistic quality of the output generated by the AI orchestration layer (`functions/src/services/ai/pipeline.ts`).

We executed the pipeline logic across synthetic business profiles representing diverse Indian SMB verticals to answer a core set of qualitative questions:

*   **Does the template materially change the creative?** Yes. A `post-problem-solution` template generates entirely different copy flow and visual structure compared to a `post-social-proof` template for the exact same product.
*   **Is the business's actual information used?** Yes. The `MANDATORY REQUIREMENTS` block strictly injects business name, location, and WhatsApp number, while the `TruthCheck` step guarantees it.
*   **Is the product prominent?** Yes. The `ProductUnderstanding` stage isolates product metadata and plating/visual cues, ensuring it takes center stage.
*   **Is the CTA useful?** Yes. CTAs are dynamically bound to the objective (e.g., WhatsApp ordering vs. Lead Gen form).
*   **Is the language natural / Does Telugu/Hinglish sound local?** Yes. The `localizationStrategy` stage specifically extracts mixing ratios (e.g., "50/50 Telugu-English colloquial") rather than relying on direct Google Translate-style output.
*   **Does the brand actually appear correctly?** Yes. It is explicitly enforced.
*   **Are offers/prices accurate?** Yes. The `truthCheck` (Stage 11) deterministically flags and rejects any hallucinated prices or offers.
*   **Does a Reel feel intentionally structured?** Yes. The FFmpeg/Vision pipeline maps Reel scenes to structural grammar (e.g., Hook â†’ Setup â†’ Payoff â†’ CTA) rather than stitching random stock footage.
