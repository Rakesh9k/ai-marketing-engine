## 2. Core Capabilities & Architectural Design

### **A. Fact Storage & Provenance Model (`factSources.ts`, `businessCreation.ts`)**
Business Brain categorizes business knowledge into explicit provenance tiers:
1. **`USER_CONFIRMED`:** Facts directly supplied or approved by the business owner during onboarding or settings edits.
2. **`DISCOVERED`:** Facts extracted automatically via web scraping, Google Places, or initial prompt ingestion.
3. **`INFERRED`:** Strategic inferences generated by AI reasoning.
4. **`UNKNOWN`:** Explicitly identified missing information.

### **B. Forbidden Claims & Compliance (`prohibited_claims`)**
Every business profile maintains a `prohibited_claims` string array. When generating campaign copy, `truthCheck.ts` verifies generated claims against this list to prevent legal compliance violations (e.g. false discount claims, unauthorized medical/financial promises).

### **C. Tenant Isolation & Ownership**
Business documents in `/businesses/{businessId}` enforce single-owner user bindings (`userId == request.auth.uid` or `hasBusinessAccess(businessId)`). Client direct writes are strictly disabled in `firestore.rules`; updates must execute through Cloud Functions (`createBusiness`, `updateBusiness`, `updateBusinessBrain`).

## 4. Onboarding & Draft Integration (`onboarding_drafts/{userId}`)

During onboarding, user progress is continuously saved to the `/onboarding_drafts/{userId}` collection via the `saveOnboardingDraft` Cloud Function:
- **Auto-Save Queue (`useDraftAutoSave.ts`):** Debounces form input changes to prevent data loss on page refresh.
- **Draft Parity & Verification (`onboardingDraftParity.test.ts`):** Tests confirm that draft fields map 1:1 to final Business Brain documents when `confirmOnboardingBusiness` executes.
- **Fact Discovery (`discoverOnboardingFacts.ts`):** Uses Gemini AI to auto-enrich sparse business inputs (e.g. extracting menu items or location benefits from a business name and city).

---

## 5. Downstream Integration & Fact Ingestion Test Results

- **Context Retrieval (`creativeContextSelector.ts`):** Formats Business Brain facts, product details, and brand tone into standard prompt context strings.
- **Verification Tests (`pipeline.informationFlow.test.ts`):** Unit tests verify that modifying a product price or brand voice in Business Brain immediately alters downstream copy generation.
- **Missing Field Handling (`pipeline.sparseFacts.test.ts`):** When optional facts are missing (e.g., no explicit discount rate), Creative Brain generates brand awareness copy without inventing fake discounts.

## 6. Recommendations & Enhancements

1. **Multi-Location Business Support:** Expand `businessBrain` schema to support multi-branch location data per business entity.
2. **Fact Freshness Verification:** Implement periodic automated prompts suggesting users re-verify outdated pricing or seasonal offers after 90 days.
