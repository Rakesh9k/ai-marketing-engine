Generated as part of the 2026-09-22 legal/compliance remediation pass. Each item below is a
decision engineering cannot make on its own — either because it requires a business/legal fact
this repository has no evidence of, or because it requires a policy choice with real financial or
legal consequence. None of these has been decided _for_ the founder anywhere in the codebase or in
the public `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/refund`, or `/cancellation` pages — each page explicitly says
"founder decision required" at the relevant point rather than inventing an answer.
